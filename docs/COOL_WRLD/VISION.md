# COOL WRLD - THE VISION

> *By the Grace of God, building tomorrow.*

---

## What is Cool Wrld?

Cool Wrld is a **creative universe** that captures, celebrates, and amplifies the unique soul of Kamloops. It's not just a brand—it's a movement, a community, and a proof of concept for what's possible when art, culture, and technology converge.

**Studio**: Odubo (production company, artist, creator)
**Universe**: Cool Wrld (the world we're building)

---

## The Origin

Everything starts with **Loops Soul**—the serialized web series and live event ecosystem that serves as the origin story, the content engine, and the community catalyst.

Loops Soul captures the intersection of music, fashion, and dance in Kamloops. Each event is designed to be filmed as an episode. Each episode is a chapter in the story. And every person who RSVPs becomes part of the founding of something bigger.

### Event Partners

Events may have **sponsors and partners** (like Valcee for fashion) who participate in individual episodes. These partnerships are separate from the Cool Wrld universe—sponsors are external collaborators, not part of the world-building.

> **Note**: Valcee is a fashion sponsor for Loops Soul events. The "Shop the Look" feature links to Valcee's store. This is a commercial partnership, not part of Cool Wrld content ownership.

## The Philosophy

### Faith & Community

The host script opens with:
> *"I come to you in the name of Jesus Christ..."*

This isn't just a line—it's the foundation. Cool Wrld is built on faith, community, and the belief that art can elevate. We're not building content; we're building a **cultural time capsule** for Kamloops.

### Kam! Loops Soul!

The signature chant is the **brand sound**. It's the moment where individuals become a collective. It should appear everywhere—in clips, in episodes, in merchandise, in the community wall. It becomes the audio logo, the emotional anchor.

### Mise-en-Scène

Every event is **choreographed but authentic**. The cameras are present, but the moments are real. This is the tension that makes Loops Soul unique: documentary authenticity meets production quality.

## Founding Members

### Who They Are

Founding Members are the people who **lock in early**—RSVPs to Episode 1, contributors to the first galleries, sharers of the first clips. They are the foundation of Cool Wrld.

### How They Feel

- **Part of something**: Their contribution matters; they're not just attendees
- **Early access**: They see things before everyone else
- **Recognized**: Their profile has the Founding Member badge
- **Connected**: They're in the group chat, the Discord, the early emails

### What They Get

**Badge**: "Founding Member" visible only to them on their profile

**Perks** (to define):
- Early access to episodes
- Exclusive behind-the-scenes content
- First look at new releases
- Founding member-only events
- Personal invite links (future)

**Recognition**:
- Season credits at the END of episodes (like Marvel post-credits)
- No public leaderboard
- Opt-in to be credited by name

### The Number

Every Founding Member gets a personal identifier (#001, #002, etc.) but it's private. This prevents the "100 founding members" issue while still giving each person a sense of personal ownership.

## Account Architecture

### Cool Wrld Accounts (Community)

Built on **Clerk** for industry-standard authentication.

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Auth** | Clerk (Google, Apple, Email magic link) |
| **Profile** | Email, phone, Instagram, avatar |
| **Access** | MyMoments, RSVPs, Cool Points, Community |
| **Identity** | Separate from Odubo admin accounts |

### Shopify Integration (Commerce)

Powered by existing Shopify store.

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Auth** | Shopify Customer accounts |
| **Access** | Purchases, orders, shipping |
| **Identity** | Odubo commerce identity |

### Email-Based Linking

Same email = linked accounts (automatic).

```
Cool Wrld User: john@email.com
        ↓ (same email)
Shopify Customer: john@email.com
        ↓ (auto-linked)
Unified Profile:
├── Community: RSVPs, Points, MyMoments
├── Commerce: Orders, Purchases, History
└── VIP Status: Based on engagement + purchases
```

### Linking Flow

1. **User signs into Cool Wrld** (Clerk)
2. **System checks Shopify** for same email
3. **If match found** → Accounts linked automatically
4. **User sees unified profile** with both community + commerce history

### Linking Benefits

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Unified Profile** | See community + commerce history in one place |
| **VIP Tiers** | "Loyal Supporter" badge based on engagement |
| **Purchase Bonuses** | Bonus Cool Points on store orders |
| **Exclusive Access** | Past purchasers unlock special rewards |

### Privacy

- Linking is automatic (same email)
- Users can unlink in settings
- Purchase history visible to user only
- No payment data shared (Shopify handles securely)

### Existing Customers

- Invite to create Cool Wrld account via email
- Auto-link if email already exists in Shopify
- No auto-creation of Cool Wrld accounts

## MyMoments

### What It Is

**MyMoments** is every attendee's personal vault and creative toolkit. It's the **gateway to the Cool Wrld universe** — the hook that turns event attendees into app users, and app users into community members.

### The Experience

**Pre-Event:**
- RSVP unlocks MyMoments access
- "Your moment awaits" anticipation messaging
- Early access to event details

**Event Night:**
- Capture photos and videos
- Upload directly to personal vault
- See real-time: "47 others have captured tonight"
- Live moments wall on screens

**Post-Event:**
- Vault unlocks with all captures
- See what everyone captured
- Creator tools to make content
- Share to social

### Creator Tools

**Audio (Odubo Music Only):**
- Sync to Odubo's tracks
- Sound effects library
- Audio enhancement (EQ, reverb, effects)

**Formatting:**
- One-tap resize for TikTok, Reels, Shorts
- Branded templates
- Filters and effects

**No Third-Party Music:**
- Copyright protection
- Only Odubo's catalog available
- Audio enhancement tools instead

## Cool Points

### The Economy

Cool Points are the **engagement currency** of Cool Wrld. They turn casual attendees into invested community members.

### Earning Points

| Action | Points |
|--------|--------|
| RSVP to event | +10 |
| Check in to event | +25 |
| Upload photo/video | +15 |
| Share to social | +20 |
| Content repurposed by others | +50 |
| Someone uses your repurposed content | +100 |
| First to 10 shares | +50 bonus |
| Engage with content (comments, etc.) | +5 |

### Redeeming Points

| Reward | Points Required |
|--------|-----------------|
| Event ticket discount | 500 |
| Exclusive BTS content | 250 |
| Merch (stickers, etc.) | 100-500 |
| Early access to episodes | 750 |
| Limited edition drops | 1000+ |

### Points Value

**Cool Points are NOT 1:1 with dollars.**

Points are earned through engagement, not purchases. They represent community participation, creativity, and contribution to Cool Wrld.

| Earned Through | Points Value |
|---------------|--------------|
| RSVPs | Community participation |
| Check-ins | Attendance |
| Uploads | Content creation |
| Shares | Viral reach |
| Repurposing | Creative reuse |

### Launch Strategy

**Earned Only (Launch Phase):**
- Bootstrap engagement
- Build community value
- Establish points economy
- No dollar-to-points conversion

**Purchase Option (Later):**
- Buy points for convenience (non-refundable)
- Merch bundles
- Premium features
- Points remain engagement-focused, not dollar-backed
