# Business Launch Checklist

Pre-launch requirements for Odubo Studio (Kamloops, BC, Canada)

---

## Business Registration

- [ ] **Register Business Name** with BC Registry Services
  - Name: "Odubo Studio" (or preferred name)
  - Cost: ~$40
  - URL: https://www.bcregistry.gov.bc.ca
  - Timeline: Instant online

- [ ] **Check City of Kamloops Business License Requirements**
  - Home-based business license may be required
  - URL: https://www.kamloops.ca/business-development
  - Cost: Varies (~$100-200/year typically)

---

## Tax Registration

- [ ] **Get a Business Number (BN)** from CRA
  - Required for any tax accounts
  - URL: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/registering-your-business.html
  - Cost: Free
  - Timeline: Instant online or 1-2 weeks by mail

- [ ] **GST/HST Registration** (optional until $30k revenue)
  - Required once revenue exceeds $30,000 in 4 consecutive quarters
  - Can register voluntarily to claim input tax credits
  - Rate: 5% GST in BC

- [ ] **BC PST Registration** (if selling taxable goods)
  - Physical products shipped to BC are generally PST taxable (7%)
  - Digital products: Generally PST exempt
  - URL: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/sales-taxes/pst

---

## Banking & Finance

- [ ] **Open Business Bank Account**
  - Keeps personal and business finances separate
  - Required for professional payment processing
  - Bring: Business registration, ID

- [ ] **Set Up Bookkeeping System**
  - Options: Wave (free), QuickBooks, FreshBooks, or spreadsheet
  - Track all income and expenses from day one

- [ ] **Payment Processing**
  - Shopify Payments (already integrated)
  - Verify business details are correct in Shopify admin

---

## Legal & Compliance

- [ ] **Privacy Policy** on website
  - Required for collecting customer data
  - Shopify has templates, or use a generator

- [ ] **Terms of Service** on website
  - Covers purchases, refunds, disputes

- [ ] **Refund/Return Policy**
  - Must be clearly stated before purchase
  - Physical goods: Consider 14-30 day policy
  - Digital goods: Can be more restrictive

- [ ] **Shipping Policy** (for physical goods)
  - Delivery times, shipping costs, regions served

---

## Before First Sale - Final Checks

- [ ] Business name registered
- [ ] Business bank account open
- [ ] Payment processing verified (test transaction)
- [ ] Privacy policy published
- [ ] Terms of service published
- [ ] Refund policy published
- [ ] Bookkeeping system ready
- [ ] First product listed correctly

---

## After First Sale

- [ ] Issue receipt/invoice to customer
- [ ] Record sale in bookkeeping
- [ ] Set aside ~15-25% for taxes (income + sales tax if applicable)
- [ ] Keep all records for 6 years (CRA requirement)

---

## Future Considerations (Not Required for Launch)

- [ ] **Trademark Registration** - Protect "Odubo" and "BAAD" names ($350+ each)
- [ ] **Incorporation** - Consider if revenue grows significantly
- [ ] **Business Insurance** - Liability protection
- [ ] **Accountant** - Recommended once revenue is consistent
