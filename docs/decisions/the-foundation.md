## The decisions

1. **Odubo Studio is a design company.** It makes tools people use to create.
   Out of the tools come works that feed the shared pool (templates, figure
   packs, stems) and art that inspires the people using them. Creative wear
   (B.A.A.D) is where tool and art meet.
2. **Product one is the home for an event's whole life**, sold to event
   curators who already have crowds. Its seven parts are Plan, Create, Asset
   home, Host, Post, Portfolio (for hosts) and Memory gallery (for guests and
   the public), built from the Loop Soul and Moments infrastructure. The
   product name is the owner's to choose.
3. **The wedge rule.** The first paid version fixes one painful job end to
   end. Ten curator conversations choose which: planning, hosting or posting.
   The first three events are set up by hand; curator accounts get built only
   after three curators have paid.
4. **The security repair lands before any other curator's guests use it.**
   `/api/customers/[id]` had no auth check (being fixed in its own session).
5. **Parked, not deleted:**
   - The figure and film service as its own product. It lives on as product
     one's signature feature.
   - The stem player as a product. It keeps serving the album.
   - The release factory as a product. Its storage becomes the asset home.
   - Magazine royalties, Cool Points, the BI dashboards, Command Center,
     livestreaming, a native app.
   - /fly, until the album rollout is done.
6. **Folded into product one:** the Ark becomes Plan, and social posting
   (Arsenal/PostForMe) becomes Post.
7. **The team:**
   - Three founding seats with ownership that vests over 4 years with a
     1-year cliff: the founder (creative and product), a technical lead
     (recruit first), and a partnerships lead (recruit second).
   - An event crew paid per event out of the curator's fee.
   - An unpaid advisory circle that meets monthly.
   - Granting ownership means incorporating first.
8. **The Leap Test, decided on 2027-07-01 with the advisory circle.** The
   owner goes full-time only when all of these are true:
   - six months of living costs are saved
   - Odubo revenue covers at least half of monthly costs, three months running
   - curators are coming back
   - both lead seats are filled
   - the advisors agree
9. **The campus is a future idea, separate from Odubo Studio.** It draws on
   IB-style inquiry, Disney-style wonder, and Christ (rooted in Him, open to
   all). It starts on conditions, not dates.
10. **Operating rules:**
    - No building without a buyer.
    - Every cost gets a real quote before it's spent.
    - One product at a time.

## Boundary

The owner's day job is not part of Odubo Studio. Its only past connection was
the venue partnership for the archived 10 October night. Keep the workplace
and its people out of business plans.

## Why

As of 2026-10-02, outside customers had paid for nothing:
- 2 passes sold, both bought by the owner
- 0 garments sold
- about 2 real customer emails in a year

Meanwhile the platform held about 790 commits of tools with one user. Selling
the tools to curators who already have crowds removes the need to build an
audience first.
