**The commercial frame (locked, from the Scott's deal):** Scott's provides the
venue, staff support, and keeps 100% of beverage revenue. **Odubo is
responsible for ticket sales, equipment, and every other cost.** Entry is $5,
unlimited tickets — see the worklist for why. That means **there is currently
no meaningful revenue line**: 100 people at $5 is $500. Budget accordingly —
this event runs on what's already sunk (existing gear, existing relationships)
plus whatever cash the owner puts in, not on ticket income.
