# 18 posts — Sept 9 → Sept 26

**Written 2026-09-08.** Companion to
[loop-soul-final-stretch.md](loop-soul-final-stretch.md) and
[loop-soul-the-ask.md](loop-soul-the-ask.md).

**One post a day. Ten minutes. Shot on a phone.** Every entry below is already
written — open the day, shoot the one thing, paste the caption, close the app.

Three rules that matter more than any caption here:

1. **Repetition is the strategy.** Most people need to see this four or five
   times before it registers as a real event they could attend. You will feel
   like you're repeating yourself long before your audience does.
2. **Your face and voice out-perform every designed asset you own.** People
   come for a person. The flyer confirms the details; you are the reason.
3. **The date, the place, and the $5 go in every single caption.** Every one.
   A post that doesn't say when and where is a post that did nothing.

Standing details for any caption: **Sat Sept 26 · Scott's Inn & Suites,
Kamloops · doors 6:30, album at 8 · $5 at the door · 19+ · odubostudio.com/loop**

## When a day gets eaten by a shift

Post the flyer with the details and nothing else. That counts. A day with a
plain flyer beats a day with nothing, every time, and the plan is built to
survive missed days — it is not built to survive being abandoned because one
day was missed.
