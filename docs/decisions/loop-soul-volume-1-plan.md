   must see what they're buying into: the anthem, the program, the Danceyokey

   matches the current engraved look (it shares the palette, but verify on a
   real device).
4. **Posters + ticket art** — needed for print and for the Shopify listing.
5. **Email sender** — code emails only reach the owner until a domain is
   verified; the lookup page covers it, but every touchpoint should say so.

### Gaps to close before the night
6. **Door kit + rehearsal of the app flow** (host + door on real phones).
7. **Wallet pass / keepsake** design.
8. **Media engine ("Loop Soul memories")** — the in-app feed of event video.

## Kamloops marketing plan

**The engine: the owner's own performances.** Record dancing/performing to his
own music through the Loop Soul filter → Instagram/TikTok → "get a pass" link.
This proves the filter, the vibe and the artist in one post, and it's content
only Loop Soul can make. Cadence: 3×/week from announcement to the event.

**Channels**
- **Instagram/TikTok** — the performance series; Danceyokey teasers ("this is
  your spot"); the anthem ("what should we all dance to?"); poster reveals.
- **Campus (TRU)** — back-to-school barbecue and first-weeks activity; posters
  and QR codes where students actually stand; a student-priced push if desired.
- **Local venues/cafés** — posters with the QR (the QR is on every poster from
  Poster Studio).
- **Affiliates/promoters** — brief #5 gives them the concept and a reason to
  bring their people; consider a per-promoter code batch so reach is measurable.
- **Scott's Inn's own audience** — the venue's guests and channels (brief #4).

**Message hierarchy:** it's not a night out, it's *a taping of a series about
Kamloops soul* — you're in the room, and you might be in the episode.

## Media distribution plan

1. **In-app (free, full music)** — Loop Soul's own media surface holds the
   event video with the real music: it's our service, our room, our record.
2. **YouTube / public** — a version with rights-safe audio (local-artist
   overdubs recorded for exactly this reason, credited).
3. **"Loop Soul memories"** — the in-app feed of everything shot at events: not
   TikTok, just the series' own memory. Every clip captured in the room feeds
   it, which is why capture and attribution matter from night one.
4. **Magazine** — the printed/digital issue per volume (see architecture doc),
   with contributor royalties.
