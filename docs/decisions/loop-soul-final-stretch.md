# Vol 1 — the final stretch (Sept 8 → Sept 26)

**Written 2026-09-08. 18 days out.** This doc exists for one reason: to make
the remaining work small enough to carry while working full time.

Everything else in `docs/decisions/loop-*` is still true. None of it is
required. Read this one when you don't know what to do next.

---

## The only thing that has to be true on Sept 26

**People are in the courtyard at 8pm when the album plays.**

That's it. That is the whole event. Every other thing that exists — the Wall,
the magazine, the filter, the poster engine, the promoter studio, the stem
field, the Warehouse, Danceyokey — is a surface built *around* an album being
played to people. If none of them ship, the night still happens exactly as
designed: doors 6:30, album 8:00, "1984" live at 9:00, out by 10:30.

## What is cut, on purpose, right now

Not "later." Cut, for Vol 1:

- The magazine / Journal issue — **it was always post-event by design**
- The Wall, the filter, the poster studio, the stem field, the Warehouse
- Digital pass artifact, selfie-onboarding, guest→poster pipeline
- Any code work that isn't the door working on the night

These are not unfinished obligations. They are **inventory** — built, paid for,
sitting on a shelf, available for Vol 2 and the volumes after. An unshipped
feature you chose not to ship is not a debt.

The only build item left: **the door works on a real phone.** One rehearsal,
one evening, week of Sept 21.

## The one number that matters

**How many people have you personally, by name, asked to come.**

Not reach. Not impressions. Not passes sold. Asked. A $5 local show 18 days out
does not fill from a funnel — it fills because Mani asked, and the person said
yes to Mani. Every promoter's first honest week looks like a contact list, not
a campaign.

If that number is under 50, marketing is not the problem. The ask is.

## The 18 days, in three moves

**1. The list (this week).** Open your phone. Write down every person who would
plausibly come — coworkers at Scott's, church, TRU, family, the band's people,
DJ Amen's people, everyone who's ever said "let me know when you play." Aim for
100 names. Send each one a message *individually*. Not a broadcast. Fifteen a
day gets you through the list in a week.

**2. The post (every day, one).** One piece of content a day for 18 days, same
message, different angle. It does not need to be good. It needs to exist and
repeat. Consistency beats production value at this scale — people need to see it
four or five times before it registers as real.

**3. Sept 11, the barbecue (3 days away).** The nearest real date. Whatever is
in hand goes in hand. If flyers aren't printed, a same-day copy shop run or a
phone screen both work. Do not let this slip into "next time."

## The daily ask

**25 minutes.** Not an evening. Not a weekend.

- 10 min — pray first, before the work, not after it
- 15 min — the list (personal messages) or the post

That's the whole commitment for 18 days. If a day gets eaten by a shift, it gets
eaten. Miss one, take the next one. The plan survives missed days; it does not
survive being too big to start.
