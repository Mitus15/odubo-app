## The engine: the personal ask

The message (no em dash, every fact in it):

> Hey [Name]. October 10 I'm playing my album live for the first time, in the
> courtyard at Scott's. Amen the DJ after, dress code is 80s. It's $5 and I
> want you there. odubostudio.com/loop

Two days later, to anyone who did not buy:

> Got yours yet? I'm counting heads for the 10th.

Rules: 25 messages a day, Sep 25 to 28, one to one before any group chat. Ask
for the purchase, never the share. Group chats on Sep 28 after the individuals.
Second wave Oct 1 to 3 with the 1984 video attached, to everyone who did not
buy. Keep the count in a note: sent, replied, bought.

Fallback if under 40 by Oct 1: a bring-one code (second pass free at
checkout) for pass-holders only, and comp the dancers. Do not lower the price.

## Amen: what to ask him

Send as one message. Items 1 to 5 are the promotion; 6 to 8 decide what the
night can promise.

1. A phone video, vertical, 20 to 30 seconds, to camera: who he is, "October
   10, the courtyard at Scott's, I'm on after the album, all 80s, $5, 19+".
   Two takes. Due Friday Sep 26. It goes up Saturday Sep 27 as a Collab post
   on both grids.
2. The three 80s songs he will definitely open the floor with. They become a
   post and a story poll.
3. His Instagram handle for the programme row on /loop, and a yes to being
   tagged and to accepting the Collab invite.
4. His push: the pass link on his story the day the Collab goes up and again
   Oct 3, and 40 messages to his own list. His number is 20 passes.
5. One line of bio and one photo for odubostudio.com/loop/press.
6. The set: is he playing under the album at 8, or only the 80s floor at 9?
   What he needs: decks, table, power, monitors, load-in time, soundcheck.
   The poster says WITH AMEN THE DJ; this is what makes it true.
7. Thirty minutes together on camera before Oct 2, both faces in frame. That
   is the cover post on Oct 2 and the only clip with both of you.
8. Names he wants on the door, or does everyone pay the $5.
