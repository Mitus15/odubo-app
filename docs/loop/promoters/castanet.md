# Castanet Kamloops (Harsha Boodhun): worth doing?

Thread: "Sponsored Content Package with Castanet Kamloops", from
harsha@castanetkamloops.net, 9, 11 and 21 September 2026.

## What was offered

- **9 Sept: the sponsored article, $500** (usually $599, prepaid). An
  interview written up as a "Standout", on the headline stack for 12 hours,
  plus one week of homepage display ads (60,000 impressions guaranteed) driving
  to it. Evergreen, reusable on your own socials, you approve it first.
- **21 Sept: two "this week" promos, $140 or $210.** The details are in the PDF
  attached to that email ("Scott's Inn & Suites.pdf"), which Claude could not
  open. Open it.
- Her file still says the event is **September 26**. It's October 10.

## The verdict

**Say no to the $500 article.** At 250 passes × $5 the night grosses $1,250 at
a sell-out. $500 is 40% of that for twelve hours on a headline stack and a week
of banner ads. The article does have value for the album later, but the album
is not for sale yet, so there is nothing for the evergreen piece to sell.

**Maybe yes to the $140 option, on three conditions.** It only earns its money
if it (1) runs in the final week, October 3 to 10, not this week, (2) links to
odubostudio.com/loop, and (3) is display or a listing that people see, not a
line in a newsletter. If the PDF says it's this week only, say no.

**The free route is better.** The story is genuinely news: a local musician
performing an unreleased album once, in a hotel courtyard, for $5, where a
guest's photo becomes the album cover. That is what a newsroom covers without
being paid. Send the press release (in the kit at odubostudio.com/loop/press)
to Castanet's news desk, CFJC and Kamloops This Week. Harsha is sales, not
editorial, so ask her for the right newsroom address or find it on their
contact page.
