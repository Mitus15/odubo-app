## The story in one paragraph

A Kamloops musician is performing his album for the first and only time, in a
hotel courtyard, for five dollars. Fourteen tracks, played live front to back
starting at eight, with Amen the DJ. The record isn't on Spotify, YouTube, or
anywhere else. October 10th is its first exhibition, and the only night it gets
played as a set.

## The night, in three parts

**6:30 · the lounge.** Fire pits, games, drinks and music in the courtyard.

**8:00 · the album, live.** All fourteen tracks, front to back, performed rather
than played back.

**9:00 · the 80s floor.** Amen takes it from the last track to the dance floor
until 10, when the courtyard closes.

## The hook

**The audience makes the album cover.** Guests shoot through the Loop Soul
filter, which renders everyone as a figure in ink on sand with no faces, and
every frame lands on the Wall, a live gallery. Everyone there votes. The
winning photograph becomes the official cover art on streaming, credited to
whoever took it, who is paid **$50**.
