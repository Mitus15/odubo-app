## A Kamloops musician is performing his album for the first and only time, in a hotel courtyard, for five dollars

**KAMLOOPS, B.C.** On Saturday, October 10, Kamloops musician Mani Odubo will
perform his album *Loop Soul* live, from beginning to end, outdoors in the
courtyard at Scott's Inn & Suites. It will be the first time anyone outside the
studio has heard it, and the only time the record gets played as a set. It is
not on Spotify, not on YouTube, and not anywhere else. A pass is five dollars,
and it doubles as a pre-order of the album.

The night runs in three parts. From 6:30 the courtyard is a lounge: fire pits,
games, drinks, music. At eight, Odubo performs the album live, front to back,
with Amen the DJ. At nine it turns into an 80s dance floor until the courtyard
closes at ten.

The lead single, "1984", plays free at odubostudio.com/loop/1984.

### The cover belongs to whoever takes it

*Loop Soul* does not yet have a settled cover. Guests shoot through a photo
filter built into the event's app, ink on sand and no faces, and every frame
lands in a live gallery. Everyone there votes. The winning photograph becomes
the album's official cover art on streaming services, credited to the person
who took it, who is paid $50.

### About Odubo Studio

Odubo Studio is the Kamloops creative practice of Emmanuel "Mani" Odubo,
working across music, fashion and live events. *Loop Soul* is its first album
and the first of a planned series of volumes, each built around a night, a
record, and the people who were there for it.
