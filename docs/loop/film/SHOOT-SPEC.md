# The shoot: Loop Soul in one take

One continuous take of you dancing the whole album, start to finish. Everything
else is made from it: the film, the chapters, every clip. The better the take
holds still around you, the cleaner the silhouette.

## The camera

- On a tripod at chest height. Locked. Nobody touches it until the end.
- 4K UHD (3840x2160), 30 fps, shutter 1/60.
- Focus, exposure and white balance all on manual and locked. Auto anything
  flickers the wall, and the flicker ends up in the cut-out.
- HEVC. The whole album is about 52 minutes, about 10 GB.
- Power plugged in, airplane mode on, no case, out of the sun. Clear 30 GB first.

## The frame

- Your whole body with half a body of space above your head, for the jumps.
- Your feet and the floor line always in frame. Your shadow on the ground is
  made from where your feet touch it.
- Tape a box on the floor for yourself, with room to every edge of the frame.
  Stay inside it.

## The room

- One plain wall and floor that do not move. Any colour that differs from your
  clothes.
- No TV, no windows, no mirrors, nothing that moves or changes light behind you.

## The light

- A soft light from one side (45 degrees), and a weaker one from the other.
- Your body needs light AND shade. The highlight cuts in the silhouette are made
  from the brightness on you: flat light makes a flat shape.

## Clothes

- Matte, mid-tone, fitted enough to read your shape. The piece can be worn; the
  badge is added over your heart in the edit.

## The sound

- Play `Loop Soul - play this while you dance.m4a` (in your media folder, under
  `film`) out loud from a speaker next to the camera, from the very top. It
  starts with a tone.
- Clap once in frame after the tone, before Welcome.
- The camera's own microphone on. The edit listens to it to line every song up
  to the picture.

## The story, in the body

- **Welcome.** Start on the floor. Rise with the build: the incarnation, a
  living soul out of the dust.
- **Welcome's last hit.** Jump. That is the take-off. The badge lands on your
  heart there.
- Then fly through the album.
- **After Ghost World.** Hold your last pose for five seconds before you move.

## Before the real take

1. Film five minutes exactly as the real take: same spot, light, clothes, the
   playlist playing.
2. Run `npm run film:check -- "<the test take>"`. It checks the camera, hears
   the tone, locks Welcome, checks your framing, the floor, the wall and the
   light, and says what to fix.
3. Fix what it says. Then dance the album. Twice if you have it in you.
