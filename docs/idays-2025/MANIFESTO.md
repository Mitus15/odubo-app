# odubo studio @ IDAYS.TRU

## A Manifesto for International Days 2025

**Thompson Rivers University | March 13, 2025**

---

## The Vision

This is not a performance. This is a movement.

When people leave International Days 2025, they will not remember watching a show. They will remember being part of something - a communal experience that began with a single drumbeat at Old Main and crescendoed into a declaration of faith at Old Gym. They will remember running together. Singing together. Witnessing something that felt more like a service than a showcase.

The goal is simple: transform what could be another university event into a cinematic, spiritually-grounded experience. An event that honors the diversity of cultures while pointing toward something transcendent. An event that people talk about not because of production value, but because of how it made them feel.

This is art as ministry. Commerce as mission. Performance as praise.

## The Experience

### Act I: The Build-Up at Old Main

**The Setup (Frank Ocean "Endless" Style)**

Before anyone realizes what's happening, a figure walks to the center of Old Main carrying a drum kit. Piece by piece, they assemble it. They sit down. They begin to play.

A rhythm emerges. Slow at first. Building.

Then another person arrives carrying a bass. They plug in. They join the groove.

A keyboardist follows. Then a guitarist. Then a saxophonist.

Each musician enters one by one, adding their voice to the growing sound. The audience gathers organically, drawn by curiosity. What is this? Who are these people?

Then the singers arrive. One voice. Then two. Then a choir materializes, harmonizing with the band. The sound swells.

Finally, a lead singer steps forward. They perform one song - a moment of pure artistry.

**The Transition: The Chants**

When the song ends, the energy shifts. The lead singer begins a call:

> *I don't know but I've been told*

The crowd responds:

> *I don't know but I've been told*

> *Our day show is up the road*

> *Our day show is up the road*

> *If you're just passing, if you just heard*

> *If you're just passing, if you just heard*

> *Come with us and spread the word*

> *Come with us and spread the word*

The rhythm pounds. The call continues:

> *What's that sound?*

> *What's that sound?*

> *What's the sound I heard, it's going down*

> *What's the sound I heard, it's going down*

The crowd joins in:

> *Whoa-oh-oh-oh-oh! Hey!*
> *Whoa-oh-oh-oh-oh! Hey!*

And then, without warning, the band starts moving. The choir starts moving. Everyone starts jogging.

### Act II: The Jog

**From Old Main to Old Gym**

This is the unexpected moment. The moment that makes this event different from anything else.

The entire ensemble - band, choir, crew, audience who choose to join - begins jogging together from Old Main to Old Gym. Not walking. Jogging. Like soldiers. Like athletes. Like people on a mission.

The chants continue as they run:

> *Whoa-oh-oh-oh-oh! Hey!*
> *Whoa-oh-oh-oh-oh! Hey!*

It's exercise. It's community. It's a parade that moves with purpose.

*Reference: Michael Jackson's "Dangerous" video - the precision, the energy, the collective movement.*

A disclaimer will be given: "You don't have to run. But if you want to join us, let's go."

During the jog, the band's instruments are being transported by setup crew who left early. By the time the joggers arrive, the stage at Old Gym will be ready.

Water stations wait at the entrance of Old Gym. Participants catch their breath. They've just done something unexpected. They're buzzing.

### Act III: The Main Event at Old Gym

**The Opening Set**

The band reassembles on stage. Different artists - people we want to highlight - perform songs. The energy from the jog carries into the room.

**The Host Handoff**

After the opening set, the host is introduced. They take over the evening's formal program - the scheduled IDAYS performances, presentations, and showcases.

Everyone who participated - musicians, choir, crew - is wearing odubo studio. Not uniformly dressed, but each person wearing something from the collection. Different pieces. Different styles. All part of the same movement.

**The Fashion Show**

The fashion show is integrated into the program. Models walk wearing odubo studio pieces. Photographers capture every look. Videographers document the full show.

This is not separate from the event - it IS the event. The fashion show is content. The content becomes social media. The social media becomes reach. The reach becomes influence.

### Act IV: The Finale

**Part 1: The Dance**

The stage clears. A single spotlight.

I step forward to perform a song - vocals only, raw, unaccompanied by band.

But I am not alone. Ximena enters.

She dances. Full choreography. A complete artistic interpretation of the song. Her movement tells the story that my voice cannot.

This is intimate. This is art. This is two people communicating through different mediums but speaking the same language.

**Part 2: The Crescendo**

The song ends. Silence.

Then the choir enters. The band returns. The stage fills.

The arrangement of "All Glory Be To Christ" begins - the New Year's tune version, but elevated. Electric. Maybe strings.

The lyrics build:

> *Unless the Lord doth build the house*
> *In vain the builders strive*
> *Should nothing of our efforts*
> *Make no legacy survive*
>
> *Unless the Lord doth build the house*
> *In vain the builders strive*

Then I step forward for my verse. And then the chorus - the full, overwhelming chorus:

> *All glory be to Christ our King*
> *All glory be to Christ*
> *His ruling reign will ever sing*
> *All glory be to Christ*

The sound swells. The lights rise. The crescendo hits.

**The Benediction**

And then - stillness.

A voice offers a blessing. A benediction. Not preachy. Not forced. Simply a moment of grace to close what has been a sacred experience.

The cameras capture this. The film ends here. In the quiet. In the reverence. In the acknowledgment that everything we've done tonight was never about us.

## Spiritual Foundation

This event is, at its core, an act of worship disguised as a cultural celebration.

The Scripture teaches: *"If you ask anything in my name, I will do it."* (John 14:14)

This project is an asking. It is a prayer made manifest through action. Every detail - from the drumbeat at Old Main to the benediction at Old Gym - is an offering.

The goal is not to proselytize. The goal is to demonstrate. To show what happens when art is made for a purpose beyond self-promotion. To create something so excellent, so beautiful, so moving that people cannot help but ask: "Why? Why go to this effort? What drives this?"

And the answer, when they dig deep enough, is faith.

The ending - "All Glory Be To Christ" followed by a benediction - is not a pivot. It is a revelation. The whole evening has been building to this. The cultures celebrated, the art displayed, the community formed - all of it was pointing here.

This is the Gospel of John approach: start with the beautiful, the interesting, the compelling. Let people follow. And at the end, reveal the truth that was present all along.
