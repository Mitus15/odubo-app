## What Is This?

We're creating a cinematic live experience for **International Days 2025** at Thompson Rivers University - and we need talented people to make it happen.

This isn't a typical campus event. It's a multi-location performance that starts at Old Main, moves through campus, and culminates in a full production at Old Gym featuring live music, fashion, and a finale that will be filmed as a short documentary.

**Date:** March 13, 2025
**Location:** Old Main → Old Gym, TRU Kamloops

---

## The Experience

**1. Old Main (The Build-Up)**
- Band assembles one instrument at a time
- Choir joins, performers sing
- Call-and-response chants build energy

**2. The Jog**
- Everyone jogs together from Old Main to Old Gym
- Chants continue during the run
- Creates unforgettable shared moment

**3. Old Gym (Main Event)**
- Opening musical performances
- Fashion show featuring BAAD clothing
- Finale: choreographed dance + full choir performance

Everything will be documented by professional videographers and photographers to create lasting content.
