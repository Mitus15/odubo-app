## Project Purpose

### Vision Statement
Transform International Days 2025 into a cinematic, multi-location experience that celebrates cultural diversity through live music, fashion, and community movement, culminating in professional content that serves both the BAAD brand and the TRU community.

### Business Case
- Establish BAAD brand presence in the Kamloops community
- Create professional content portfolio for future use
- Build relationships with TRU World and university administration
- Demonstrate capability for large-scale event production
- Unite diverse talents around a shared creative vision

---

## Objectives

### Primary Objectives
1. Execute a coordinated three-location experience (Old Main → Transit → Old Gym)
2. Produce a fashion show featuring 8-15 models
3. Deliver a professional musical finale with band and choir
4. Capture high-quality video and photo content throughout

### Secondary Objectives
1. Build a reliable team for future events
2. Establish BAAD @ [Event] model for replication
3. Create lasting content (documentary, clips, photos)
4. Strengthen TRU World relationship

## Constraints

| Constraint | Description | Impact |
|------------|-------------|--------|
| **Date Fixed** | March 13, 2025 - cannot be moved | All planning works backward from this |
| **Budget Limited** | Self-funded with limited resources | Must prioritize spending |
| **Volunteer Team** | No paid positions | Motivation through other means |
| **Venue Dependent** | Requires TRU approval | Must secure permissions early |
| **Weather Dependent** | Outdoor jog segment | Need contingency plan |

## Assumptions

| Assumption | Risk if Invalid |
|------------|-----------------|
| TRU World approves proposal | Project may need to be scaled down |

## Risk Register

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| TRU denies permission | Medium | High | Early submission, relationship building |
| Insufficient team members | Medium | High | Early recruitment, multiple channels |
| Key person drops out | Medium | Medium | Cross-training, backup performers |
| Equipment failure | Low | High | Backup equipment, sound check |
| Weather issues for jog | Medium | Medium | Contingency indoor route |
| Rehearsal attendance poor | Medium | Medium | Clear expectations, commitment |
| Content quality insufficient | Low | Medium | Multiple videographers, quality check |
| Budget overrun | Medium | Medium | Prioritize spending, track costs |

## Change Control

Changes to scope, schedule, or budget must be:
1. Documented in writing
2. Reviewed by Project Lead
3. Approved by Project Sponsor (if significant)
4. Communicated to affected team members

**Significant changes include:**
- Addition or removal of major program elements
- Schedule changes affecting multiple teams
- Budget increases over $100
