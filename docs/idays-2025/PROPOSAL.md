# BAAD @ IDAYS.TRU

## Partnership Proposal for International Days 2025

**Submitted to:** TRU World
**Submitted by:** Odubo (BAAD)
**Date:** January 2025

---

## Executive Summary

BAAD, a student-founded clothing brand at Thompson Rivers University, proposes a collaborative partnership for International Days 2025 that elevates the event through integrated live performance, fashion showcase, and professional content creation.

Our proposal offers:
- A dynamic pre-event activation at Old Main that builds energy and draws attendees to the main venue
- A fully produced fashion show featuring diverse student models
- A culminating musical performance with live band and choir
- Professional videography and photography documenting the entire event
- Lasting digital content that promotes TRU's international community

We are offering this as a **talent and content sponsorship** - bringing creative production value rather than seeking financial investment.

---

## About BAAD

**BAAD** (Bold Authentic Aesthetic Dreams) is a clothing brand founded by a current TRU student, focused on creating distinctive, quality apparel that celebrates individuality and cultural diversity.

**Brand Website:** odubo.studio

**Our Mission:** Create cultural moments that bring people together through fashion, music, and shared experience.

**Why IDAYS:** International Days celebrates the diversity of TRU's global community. BAAD's vision aligns directly with this celebration - using fashion as a universal language that transcends cultural boundaries.

## Proposed Collaboration

### Event Flow Overview

| Time | Location | Activity |
|------|----------|----------|
| Pre-Event | Old Main | Live music build-up, energy generation, crowd gathering |
| Transition | Campus | Group movement from Old Main to Old Gym |
| Main Event | Old Gym | Integration with IDAYS programming |
| Fashion Show | Old Gym | BAAD fashion showcase with student models |
| Finale | Old Gym | Musical performance with full band and choir |

### Component 1: Pre-Event Activation (Old Main)

**What:** A live musical performance that builds organically, drawing curious passersby and building anticipation for the main event.

**How:**
- Musicians assemble and begin playing
- Vocalists and choir join progressively
- Creates a "happening" that attracts attention
- Concludes with invitation to join the move to Old Gym

**Benefit to IDAYS:**
- Generates buzz before the main event
- Draws additional attendees who might not have planned to come
- Creates shareable social media moments

### Component 2: Fashion Show Integration

**What:** A runway-style fashion presentation featuring BAAD clothing worn by student models representing TRU's diverse community.

**How:**
- 8-15 student models
- Professional runway presentation
- Professional photography throughout
- Can be scheduled within IDAYS programming

**Benefit to IDAYS:**
- Adds production value to the event
- Showcases TRU student entrepreneurship
- Provides visual interest and photo opportunities
- Celebrates diversity through fashion

### Component 3: Musical Finale

**What:** A produced musical performance featuring live band, choir, and choreographed elements.

**How:**
- 5-piece band (drums, bass, keys, guitar, saxophone)
- Choir of 6-12 vocalists
- Featured dance performance
- Closing set that caps the evening

**Benefit to IDAYS:**
- Professional-quality closing performance
- Creates a memorable conclusion
- Provides high-quality video content for future IDAYS promotion

### Component 4: Content Creation

**What:** Professional documentation of the entire event for both BAAD and TRU use.

**Deliverables:**
- 2-3 videographers capturing full event
- 2 photographers documenting fashion and performances
- Edited video content (highlight reel, full documentary)
- High-resolution photos

**Benefit to IDAYS:**
- Professional promotional content at no cost
- Material for future IDAYS marketing
- Social media content showcasing TRU's international community
