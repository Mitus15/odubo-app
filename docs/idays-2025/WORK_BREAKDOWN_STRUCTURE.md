# Work Breakdown Structure

## BAAD @ IDAYS.TRU

---

## Overview

This document decomposes all work required for the BAAD @ IDAYS.TRU project into manageable tasks organized by phase and category.

**WBS Levels:**
- **Level 1:** Major Phase (1.0, 2.0, etc.)
- **Level 2:** Work Package (1.1, 1.2, etc.)
- **Level 3:** Task (1.1.1, 1.1.2, etc.)
- **Level 4:** Subtask (1.1.1.1, etc.)

---

## 2.0 Event Day - Old Main

### 2.1 Setup

#### 2.1.1 Arrival & Preparation
- 2.1.1.1 Arrive at Old Main (2 hours before start)
- 2.1.1.2 Unload equipment
- 2.1.1.3 Setup crew positions equipment
- 2.1.1.4 Sound check
- 2.1.1.5 Confirm all performers present

#### 2.1.2 Staging
- 2.1.2.1 Position drum kit
- 2.1.2.2 Position bass amp
- 2.1.2.3 Position keyboard
- 2.1.2.4 Position guitar amp
- 2.1.2.5 Position vocal microphones
- 2.1.2.6 Final sound level check

---

### 2.2 Performance

#### 2.2.1 The Build-Up
- 2.2.1.1 Drummer enters and begins playing
- 2.2.1.2 Bassist enters and joins
- 2.2.1.3 Keyboardist enters and joins
- 2.2.1.4 Guitarist enters and joins
- 2.2.1.5 Saxophonist enters and joins
- 2.2.1.6 First vocalist enters
- 2.2.1.7 Choir enters progressively
- 2.2.1.8 Lead singer performs song

#### 2.2.2 The Chants
- 2.2.2.1 Lead chant #1 ("I don't know but I've been told...")
- 2.2.2.2 Lead chant #2 ("What's that sound...")
- 2.2.2.3 "Whoa" chant with movement initiation

---

### 2.3 Transition

#### 2.3.1 Instrument Movement
- 2.3.1.1 Setup crew begins loading instruments during chants
- 2.3.1.2 Transport instruments to Old Gym
- 2.3.1.3 Begin setup at Old Gym stage

#### 2.3.2 The Jog
- 2.3.2.1 Give disclaimer ("You don't have to jog...")
- 2.3.2.2 Begin group movement
- 2.3.2.3 Maintain chants during jog
- 2.3.2.4 Arrive at Old Gym
