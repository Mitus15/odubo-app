# Recoolman flies into the world: phase 1, the flight

**2026-09-29** · New route `/fly` (hidden, not in the menu). Branch `claude/stick-man-dodge-game-482c5b`.

## What the owner asked

Two screen recordings of a third-person flight game: a small figure dives through
low-poly spires, scoring by flying close to rock (a counter grows while hugging a
wall, then banks), a multiplier (x2 +1070), portals (+1000), crevices that open into
light. "Something like this using the stick man": Recoolman flying *into the world*,
steered on a phone by a finger sliding back and forth, subtly.

Answers given before building:

- **Look:** abstract and minimal, not the clip's rocky canyons. World building matters most.
- **Modes:** a challenge mode like the clip, *with a leaderboard*, and a relaxed mode.
  The second objective: experience the music in this world for as long as possible.
- **Placement:** a hidden link first.

The full plan (four phases, a phone check after each) is in the approved plan; the
short version: **1** the flight, **2** the song composes the world (energy maps, gates on
drops, a region per track, Drift driven by the music), **3** the leaderboard, **4** polish.

## What phase 1 built

- **Engine** (`src/components/fly/engine/`, pure TypeScript, fixed 120 Hz steps, seeded).
  `FlightEngine` flies, steers (critically damped spring toward a slide target), collides,
  and scores. `WorldDirector` composes the world 40 m at a time around a **safe line**: a
  smooth path that is always flyable, with a corridor that narrows as intensity rises.
  Patterns: field, arch, slot (the crevice: walls past both edges, one gap), portal, skim
  slabs just under the line. Scenery beyond the play area. **Obstacles may crowd the
  corridor but never close it.**
- **Rules** (`src/lib/fly/scoring.ts`, shared with the future leaderboard). 1 point per metre;
  chain grows inside 2.5 m of a surface (45/s at the edge, 460/s brushing it), banks 0.6 s
  after clearing, multiplier x2..x5 at 3/6/10/15 s; portal +1000.
- **Dive:** one touch ends it, speed 24 → 46 m/s. **Drift:** a touch is a stumble (slowed,
  ghosting through), never an end, clock instead of score. The title screen is a live
  autopilot flight; tapping a mode continues the same flight with no cut.
- **Scene** (`src/components/fly/scene/`): one instanced mesh per form kind (3 draw calls
  for the world, 8 in total), flat-shaded sand stone darkening with depth, a rim of light
  (the uniform phase 2 drives with the bass), FogExp2 into a gradient sky, noisy cloud
  billboards, GPU-wrapped dust, Recoolman in chrome black reflecting the region's own sky.
  The world is re-centred every 2 km so long Drifts do not shimmer.
- **Screens** in the house style (type and hairlines, one drawn button: Again), Jost
  throughout. Local best in `localStorage` (`fly.best.dive`).
- Site chrome: `/fly` added to the sidebar hide list and the full-width list;
  `PlayerRoot`'s music shortcuts now ignore keys a page has already handled (the flight
  steers with the arrows, which used to seek the track).

## Found along the way

- **Recoolman's rig is offset from his body.** In `public/models/avatar-male.glb` the
  skeleton sits 0.864 m to the side of the mesh. At rest nothing shows; rotate any bone and
  the limb swings about a joint in empty air. Nobody had posed a bone before (the street
  runner moves him as one rigid piece). `scene/rig.ts` moves the root bones onto the body
  and recalculates the inverses at load, on a private clone. See `docs/debug/recoolman-rig.md`.
- **The glide line and level boxes disagree.** A flat slab under a descending flight path
  gets flown *into* at its far end. Forms are therefore sheared along the glide (verticals
  stay vertical, flat faces fall with the line), which keeps path-space collision exact.
  Three's instanced normals do not survive a shear; flat shading takes normals from the
  surface, so the light stays right.
- The GDPR banner is `z-40`, under the flight's `z-50`. It never covers the game; analytics
  simply stay off on `/fly` until someone consents elsewhere on the site.

## Verified

- `npm test -- --testPathPattern=fly`: 20 passing, including six seeds of two minutes of
  Dive flown on the safe line without a touch, and restarts never beginning inside a form.
  A pilot who never steers dies in 7 to 27 s, so the world is not empty.
- `npx tsc --noEmit`: 850 errors against the 855 baseline, `TS2307` unchanged at 10, none
  in the new code. ESLint clean on the new files.
- Browser pane at 375×812: title, Dive (chain under him, `+182` bank burst), crash with the
  crane-up camera and game over, pause, Drift clock. 120 fps on the Mac, 8 draw calls,
  55k triangles.
