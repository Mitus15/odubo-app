  the translators' supplied words. Fixed properly.

- Python's certificate store cannot fetch model weights here; curl can.
- The disk is under 10 GB free: the full run and the turbo transcription wait
  on an external SSD.

  pointed at the vinyl date; pre-order is per product now.
