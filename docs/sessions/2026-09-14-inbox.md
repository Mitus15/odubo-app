# 2026-09-14: the customer inbox

## What was asked

A way to message store customers back and forth from a dedicated space, not
from the middle of Gmail. A customer-service tool inside the admin.

## What the evidence said first

A year of Gmail, searched before designing: two customer emails in twelve
months ("is your store still active?" in February, an affiliate-style question
this week) and zero contact-form submissions found. The contact form emails
`SUPPORT_EMAIL` and persists nothing, so nobody can say whether submissions
were made and lost or never made. Volume was never the problem; the missing
record was. Decision record: `docs/decisions/customer-inbox.md`.

## What was built

**Data.** `database/migrations/159_inbox.sql`: `inbox_contacts`,
`inbox_threads`, `inbox_messages`, `inbox_attachments`. Applied to remote D1
this session. Idempotency lives in unique columns (`resend_email_id`,
`submission_id`) written with `INSERT OR IGNORE`.

**Server.** `src/lib/inbox/`:
- `text.ts`: tokens, plus-addresses, Message-ID parsing, automated-mail
  detection, quoted-reply stripping, template filling. Pure; under test.
- `threads.ts`: the D1 store. Contacts by lowercase email, threads, messages,
  the admin list, the thread detail with the customer's orders (`orders` by
  email) and Loop Soul codes (`event_codes` by email).
- `resend-inbound.ts`: Svix verification by hand (base64 secret after
  `whsec_`, HMAC over `id.timestamp.body`, any `v1` entry, 5-minute window)
  and the two receiving REST calls. Under test.
- `send.ts`: the acknowledgement and every reply, with `Reply-To` carrying the
  thread token, our own `Message-ID`, and `In-Reply-To` / `References`.
- `alerts.ts`: `notifyOwner` fan-out; email today, push and SMS named for later.
- `settings.ts`: `global_settings` keys `inbox.from`, `inbox.alert_to`,
  `inbox.auto_ack`, `inbox.alert_channels_json`, `inbox.templates_json`, with
  env fallbacks `INBOX_FROM`, `INBOX_ALERT_TO`, `SUPPORT_EMAIL`.

**API.**
- `POST /api/contact` now persists first, then emails. Accepts `submissionId`.
  Returns `threadUrl`.
- `POST /api/webhooks/resend/inbound`: 401 bad signature, 200 once stored or
  already known, 503 while not (Resend retries).
- `/api/admin/inbox/threads` (list, `?count=unread`), `/threads/[id]` (GET marks
  read, PATCH status/topic/order/notes), `/threads/[id]/messages` (reply or
  note), `/threads/new` (owner starts a thread), `/templates` (GET/PUT). All
  behind `requireAdmin`.
- `/api/messages/[token]`: the customer's GET and POST, rate-limited, notes
  filtered server-side, `Cache-Control: private, no-store`.

**Surfaces.**
- `/admin/inbox`: three panes (list, conversation, customer), stacked on
  mobile. Filters and status/topic as tappable text, hairline rules, one
  primary Send. Canned replies fill `{name}` and `{order}`. Notes inline.
  "New message" starts a thread to any email; the Customers tab links here
  with `?to=&name=`.
- `/messages/[token]`: the customer's page. No login. Polls for replies.
- Store `ContactModal`: sends a `submissionId`, shows "Open your conversation"
  after submit, no longer prints the dead `info@odubo.studio`.
- Admin nav: Inbox under Commerce with an unread badge (polled each minute).
  `'inbox': 'commerce'` in `usePermissions`.

**Changed in passing.** `sendEmail` in `src/lib/email.ts` gained optional
`from`, `replyTo`, `headers`; `emailWrapper` and `ctaButton` are exported.
`jest.setup.js` guards its `window.matchMedia` mock so `@jest-environment node`
suites can run.

## Known gaps

- Push to the phone (the owner's alert choice) needs push infrastructure that
  does not exist; the alert layer is shaped for it. Phase 2.
- Whether Resend honours a custom `Message-ID` header on send is unverified.
  If it does not, In-Reply-To matching silently never fires; the plus-address
  path does not depend on it.
- 12 files still print `info@odubo.studio`; only the contact modal was fixed.
- The admin page shows the site's cookie banner over the composer until it is
  dismissed; that is the site-wide consent component, not the inbox.
