BCLC’s PlayNow.com is the only legal, regulated online sportsbook in British Columbia. Despite its monopoly, competition from offshore betting platforms continues to challenge its market presence. This section evaluates PlayNow.com’s website performance, user demographics, sports betting engagement trends, and overall strengths and weaknesses to provide a comprehensive assessment of its current footprint.

3.1 Website & App Performance

PlayNow.com has seen steady traffic, with approximately 1 million visits in February 2025, although this marked a decline of 118,000 visits compared to the previous month (Ahrefs, 2025). The platform’s user base is predominantly Canadian (88.6%), with engagement largely dependent on lottery and casino gaming rather than sports betting.

The mobile app’s performance is harder to quantify due to the lack of publicly available download figures. However, user reviews on Google Play and the App Store indicate mixed feedback, with frequent complaints about interface clunkiness, slow load times, and limited promotional offers compared to offshore platforms (SimilarWeb, 2025).

3.2 Player Demographics & Behavior

The majority of PlayNow.com users (59.5%) are male, with the largest age group being 55-64 years old (SimilarWeb, 2025).

Sports betting engagement on PlayNow.com remains relatively low compared to other gambling categories:
In 2015, only 9% of PlayNow.com users engaged in sports betting, making up just 0.1% of total bets (Lesch & Clark, 2017 – UBC Gambling Research Report).
More recent figures indicate growth in revenue, with sports betting revenue increasing from $23.9 million in 2020-21 to $31.6 million in 2022-23, a 32% increase (BIV, 2023).
High spending is concentrated among a small user base: The top 5% of sports bettors account for 66.6% of total sports betting revenue, with an average bet size of $48.88 (Lesch & Clark, 2017 – UBC Gambling Research Report).

This data suggests that sports betting remains a niche segment within PlayNow.com, largely driven by high-spending bettors rather than casual users.

4.1. Market Position & Demographics

PlayNow.com has a distinct advantage as the only legally regulated sportsbook in British Columbia (BCLC, 2024), ensuring compliance with provincial gaming laws. However, this regulatory exclusivity has not prevented bettors from seeking offshore alternatives like Bet365 and DraftKings, which provide better odds, superior technology, and a broader range of betting markets (Oddspedia, 2024).
A significant demographic gap also exists between PlayNow.com and its competitors. Bet365 and DraftKings attract a younger audience (18-44), while PlayNow.com primarily appeals to older bettors (55+) (SimilarWeb, 2024). Younger bettors tend to prefer mobile-optimized platforms, aggressive promotional offers, and advanced betting options like Same-Game Parlays (SGPs), live streaming, and esports wagering—areas where PlayNow.com lags behind.
Despite its 32% revenue growth from 2020-2023, PlayNow.com remains a secondary choice for high-frequency bettors, who favor the more flexible and competitive markets of Bet365 and DraftKings (BIV, 2023).

4.2. Sports Coverage & Betting Markets

PlayNow.com covers all major North American sports, including hockey (NHL), football (NFL), basketball (NBA), and baseball (MLB) (PlayNow, 2024). However, its limited coverage of international events and niche sports makes it less competitive compared to Bet365, which offers the widest selection of global sports, including cricket, handball, Gaelic sports, and esports (Oddspedia, 2024). DraftKings also stands out for its strong focus on American sports, along with esports, table tennis, and non-traditional markets like political betting (BleacherNation, 2024).


In terms of betting market diversity, PlayNow.com offers basic markets like moneylines, point spreads, and over/unders but lacks the depth of prop bets, niche futures, and Same-Game Parlays (SGPs) that make DraftKings and Bet365 appealing to advanced bettors (PlayNow, 2024). Bet365 provides the most expansive variety of betting options, including player prop betting, micro-betting (real-time in-play bets), and live odds enhancements (Covers, 2024).

Additionally, PlayNow.com’s odds are consistently weaker compared to its competitors, particularly in soccer, tennis, and basketball, where Bet365 regularly offers more favorable lines (Reddit Sportsbook Forum, 2024).

4.3. User Interface (UI) & User Experience (UX)

User experience is a critical differentiator in the highly competitive online sportsbook market. Bet365 and DraftKings both offer modern, user-friendly interfaces that provide seamless navigation, dynamic odds updates, and real-time game tracking, ensuring bettors stay engaged (Next.io, 2024).

DraftKings offers the best overall UX, designed with intuitive menus, personalized bet recommendations, and a smooth mobile-first experience (SportsBettingDime, 2024). Bet365 follows closely, providing a clean, efficient UI but occasionally being criticized for its text-heavy layout and outdated aesthetics (Reddit Sportsbook Forum, 2024).

In contrast, PlayNow.com struggles with an outdated UI that lacks the fluid navigation and interactive features found in its competitors. Its design is functional but uninspiring, which may deter younger bettors accustomed to more engaging platforms (PlayNow, 2024).

4.4. Live Betting & Streaming Capabilities

Live betting is a major growth area in sports wagering, and both Bet365 and DraftKings have heavily invested in this sector.
Bet365 has the best live betting experience, featuring thousands of in-play markets, real-time odds adjustments, and seamless live streaming of sporting events (SportsbookReview, 2024).
DraftKings also offers a strong live betting platform, incorporating real-time statistics (“Stats Hub”) to assist bettors but lacks the volume of live-streamed events found on Bet365 (Sportshandle, 2024).
PlayNow.com provides basic live betting but does not offer live streaming, putting it at a severe disadvantage against Bet365 (PlayNow, 2024).

For PlayNow.com to remain competitive, integrating live streaming into its platform is crucial.

Embracing Emerging Trends in Online Sports Betting

The online sports betting industry is evolving rapidly, with emerging trends shaping user expectations. Artificial intelligence (AI)-driven bet recommendations can enhance engagement by analyzing bettor behavior and offering personalized wagering suggestions (Market Research Archive, 2024). In addition, social and community betting features such as leaderboards, group betting, and community forums have been proven to increase retention and interaction among bettors (Convin, 2024).

Cryptocurrency adoption is another growing trend. Many sportsbooks are integrating blockchain-based payment solutions to offer faster, more secure transactions and appeal to a younger, tech-savvy audience (NJ Bet, 2024). While PlayNow.com currently does not support cryptocurrency, introducing digital payment methods like Bitcoin or Ethereum could expand its user base and attract younger demographics.

Another critical area of development is micro-betting, which allows users to place high-frequency, in-game wagers on events such as the next point in tennis or the next foul in basketball. This trend has grown significantly, particularly among mobile users, and is expected to dominate the sports betting landscape by 2025 (Yogonet, 2024).

Enhancing Sportsbook Features

To compete with Bet365 and DraftKings, PlayNow.com must improve its range of sports markets, betting flexibility, and overall engagement strategies.

One key improvement is the introduction of gamification elements—offering betting streak rewards, achievement badges, and interactive challenges. These features have been shown to increase user engagement and repeat betting frequency (Convin, 2024). A loyalty system with tiered rewards similar to DraftKings’ Dynasty Rewards could encourage long-term retention and make PlayNow.com more appealing to frequent bettors (Covers, 2024).

Additionally, in-game micro-betting and expanded live wagering options would provide bettors with more dynamic opportunities to engage with games as they unfold. Bet365 leads the industry in this area, offering thousands of real-time betting markets per event (Sportsbook Review, 2024). If PlayNow.com enhances its live betting experience, including cash-out features and real-time statistics integration, it can capture a share of this growing market segment.

Live streaming integration is another area where PlayNow.com lags. Bet365 and DraftKings both offer live streaming of sporting events, allowing users to watch games and place bets simultaneously (TalkSport, 2024). Adding streaming functionality would significantly enhance the in-play betting experience and help retain bettors within the PlayNow ecosystem.

Refining User Experience and Design

A modernized UI/UX is essential for PlayNow.com to compete with market leaders. Bet365 and DraftKings have invested heavily in clean, mobile-friendly, and intuitive interfaces, making navigation effortless for users (Next.io, 2024).

One of the primary criticisms of PlayNow.com is that its user interface feels outdated and clunky compared to its competitors (PlayNow, 2024). To address this, PlayNow.com should implement:
A mobile-first design that prioritizes seamless navigation on smartphones.
Personalized content and AI-driven betting recommendations that suggest markets based on user behavior (Market Research Archive, 2024).
Faster bet placement options, such as one-tap quick bets that enable users to react instantly to changing odds.

Enhancing mobile app functionality is another key priority. While Bet365 and DraftKings rank among the top-rated mobile sportsbook apps, PlayNow.com’s app has been criticized for slow response times and lack of advanced betting features (SportsBettingDime, 2024). A full app redesign incorporating biometric logins, push notifications for live odds updates, and in-app account management tools could significantly improve user experience.

Leveraging Data Partnerships for Better Betting Insights

PlayNow.com should also partner with leading sports data providers like Genius Sports or Sportradar to improve real-time analytics and odds accuracy. Bet365’s success in live betting is largely attributed to its partnerships with advanced data platforms, allowing it to offer dynamic, up-to-the-second odds and real-time player statistics (Sportsbook Review, 2024).

Integrating advanced predictive analytics and interactive betting insights would help PlayNow.com stand out, providing bettors with high-quality, data-driven wagering experiences.

Ensuring Responsible Gambling & Compliance
Given PlayNow.com’s position as a government-regulated sportsbook, responsible gambling initiatives should remain a top priority. While PlayNow.com already offers self-exclusion options and deposit limits, it should expand on this by introducing:
Personalized gambling insights that help users track their betting habits over time (Market Research Archive, 2024).
AI-driven harm detection systems that flag potentially risky betting behaviors and provide early intervention tools (Committees.Parliament, 2024).
More interactive responsible gaming features, such as customizable notifications that alert users when they have exceeded their pre-set betting limits.

Bet365 has pioneered advanced responsible gambling initiatives, leveraging AI to detect betting patterns that indicate potential problem gambling behaviors (Committees.Parliament, 2024). By integrating similar tools, PlayNow.com can reinforce its position as a leader in safe and responsible sports betting.
