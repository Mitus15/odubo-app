Team Agreement Canvas                                                                                          Team Name: Red Triangle
Purpose and Mission
Why are we doing what we are doing?
Purpose:
Our purpose is to deliver a high-quality product for BCLC that provides actionable insights and innovative solutions. Through this project, we aim to apply our academic knowledge to a real-world business challenge.
Mission:
Our mission is to work collaboratively as a team to research, analyze, and present a comprehensive project that meets BCLC’s expectations and demonstrates our ability to think critically, creatively, and strategically. 

Roles & Skills
What roles need fulfilling?
1. Project Manager: Oversees the project timeline, ensures tasks are completed, and acts as the main point of contact.
2. Researcher/Analyst: Gathers data, conducts market research, and analyzes information relevant to BCLC.
3. Creative Strategist: Develops innovative ideas, designs presentations, and ensures the project is visually appealing.
4. Editor/Quality Controller: Reviews all deliverables for accuracy, consistency, and professionalism.
What skills do we have within the team?
Strong analytical and data interpretation skills.
Excellent communication and presentation abilities.
Creative problem-solving and strategic thinking.
Proficiency in tools like Excel, PowerPoint, and project management software.
Time management and organizational skills.

Goals and Outcomes
What do we need to achieve?
Deliver a comprehensive and actionable deliverable product for BCLC.
Provide innovative solutions or recommendations that align with BCLC’s goals.
Present a professional and well-structured final report and presentation.
What does success look like?
A polished, well-researched project that meets or exceeds BCLC’s expectations.
Positive feedback from professors and product owner.
A sense of accomplishment and teamwork within the group.
How will we know we're done?
All project deliverables are submitted on time.
The final presentation is rehearsed and ready for submission.
The team agrees that all objectives have been met, and no critical tasks are pending.











Values and Norms
What are our common principles and values for our work together?
1. Collaboration: We value each team member’s input and work together to achieve the best results.
2. Respect: We listen to each other’s ideas and provide constructive feedback.
3. Accountability: Each member takes responsibility for their tasks and meets deadlines.
4. Professionalism: We maintain a high standard of work and communication.
5. Transparency: We communicate openly about challenges and progress.
Norms for our team:
Regular check-ins (several weekly meetings) to track progress.
Respond to messages within a short time.
Share workload evenly and support each other when needed.
Be punctual for meetings and deadlines.

Guidelines and Expectations
What are the rules for how we are working together?
1. Communication:
Use communication channels like WhatsApp, Teams, in-person meetings for all team discussions.
Respond to messages almost immediately to keep the project moving forward.
2. Collaboration:
Share ideas openly and encourage brainstorming.
Divide tasks based on each member’s strengths and availability.
Support each other by offering help when someone is struggling with their tasks.
3. Decision-Making:
Decisions will be made collaboratively, with input from all team members.
Document all decisions and action items to ensure clarity and accountability.
4. Continuous Improvement:
Regularly review progress during team meetings and identify areas for improvement.
Be open to feedback and willing to adapt to changes.
Reflect on what’s working well and what’s not at the end of each milestone.
5. Meeting Etiquette:
Be punctual for all meetings and come prepared with updates on your tasks.
Stick to the agenda and keep meetings focused and efficient.
If unable to attend, inform the team in advance and provide updates on your work.
6. Conflict Resolution:
Address conflicts openly and respectfully.
