Sprint 1 Review & Retrospective – BCLC Consulting Project – S03 Red Triangle
Sprint Review
What Went Well

1. Holistic Understanding of BCLC & PlayNow
   - Successfully gathered a broad overview of PlayNow’s positioning, market landscape, and competitors.
2. Multiple Deliverables Completed
   - Generated three comprehensive documents covering PlayNow’s footprint, competitor analysis, and market strategies.
3. Diverse Research & Collaboration
   - Each team member contributed research from multiple sources, allowing for a well-rounded perspective.
4. Identification of Research Gaps
   - Key areas where more depth is required were identified, particularly in competitive analysis and quantitative market data.
5. Role Specialization Moving Forward
   - Instead of all team members working on the same tasks, we now have a clearer plan to assign specific research areas.
Gaps & Challenges in Sprint 1

1. Lack of a Defined Product Backlog at the Start
   - We did not begin with a structured backlog, leading to a lack of clear dependencies and prioritization.
2. Broad Approach, Less Depth
   - Since each member attempted to conduct a full analysis independently, we lacked depth in certain areas.
3. Limited Competitor Coverage
   - We only analyzed two competitors (Bet365 & DraftKings) instead of five.
4. Minimal Quantitative Analysis
   - Research lacked numerical insights such as market share, sportsbook offerings, and comparative feature evaluations.
5. Disjointed Scrum Process
   - Backlog, sprint backlog, and team agreement were developed after the Potentially Shippable Product (PSP), reversing the intended Agile workflow.
6. Multiple Deliverables Instead of One
   - Findings were split across three separate documents instead of a single comprehensive report.
7. Lack of Explicit Scrum Roles
   - No explicitly defined Product Owner or Scrum Master, leading to a lack of structured task assignments and oversight.

Adjustments for Sprint 2
Expanded Competitive Analysis
• Identify three additional competitors in BC: Bulldog, Betway, and another BC-based sportsbook.
• Conduct in-depth quantitative research on market share, market size, sportsbook offerings, features, pricing, and promotions.
• Perform a comparative analysis evaluating PlayNow’s position against all competitors collectively.
Enhanced PlayNow Footprint Analysis
• Research player acquisition sources, advertising spend, and promotional strategies.
• Assess PlayNow’s partnerships and its impact on BC’s sports betting market.
Revised Product Backlog & Sprint Structure
• Redesign the Product Backlog to ensure it includes all aspects of research, analysis, and presentation.
• Integrate both technical and communication aspects such as report writing, visual presentation, and executive summaries.
• Ensure one consolidated deliverable at the end of Sprint 2.
Task Specialization for Sprint 2
• Assign specific research roles to each team member:
   - Current Footprint Analysis → One member refines PlayNow’s positioning with new findings.
   - Comparative Analysis → One member focuses on evaluating competitors and PlayNow’s competitive position.
   - Marketing Strategy → Two members expand on market trends, best practices, and industry innovations.
Maintaining a Holistic Approach While Deepening Research
• Continue using a broad approach while ensuring each component is researched in depth.
• Maintain focus on competitive analysis, market strategy, and PlayNow’s current footprint.
• Implementation planning and final marketing refinements will be pushed to Sprint 3.

Key Takeaways & Next Steps
• We gained a strong initial understanding of PlayNow’s position, competitors, and strategic opportunities.
• Our biggest gap was in depth—Sprint 2 will prioritize expanding competitor analysis, enhancing quantitative research, and refining PlayNow’s footprint evaluation.
• We need a structured backlog from the start to ensure efficiency in future sprints.
• Each team member will specialize in one area to ensure deeper, focused research.
• Sprint 2 deliverable will be consolidated into one comprehensive report.
