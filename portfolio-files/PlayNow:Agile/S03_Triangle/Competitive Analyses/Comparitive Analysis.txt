Comparison of Company Footprints:
The gambling industry in British Columbia (BC) is quite interesting because it has both a government-regulated platform and international gambling operators competing for users. The British Columbia Lottery Corporation (BCLC), a Crown corporation established in 1985, is the only provincially authorized gambling provider, running PlayNow.com—BC’s official legal and government-regulated online gambling platform. Through this site, users can bet on sports, play casino games, poker, and lotteries, all within a secure and regulated environment. However, despite PlayNow.com’s exclusive legal status, Bet365 and Betway, two of the largest private online gambling companies worldwide, also attract BC residents. These international platforms operate in a legal gray area, meaning they aren't directly regulated by BC authorities, but since Canada’s federal laws do not explicitly ban foreign operators, people in BC can still access them freely. Although all three platforms provide similar services, their regulations, financial impact, betting options, and overall user experience are very different.
Since PlayNow.com is government-run, it operates under strict provincial laws and is overseen by the Gaming Policy and Enforcement Branch (GPEB). This means all profits from PlayNow.com go directly back into public services like healthcare, education, and community programs. In 2023 alone, BCLC generated over $1.3 billion in revenue, a good portion of which was reinvested into BC’s economy. Additionally, PlayNow.com follows strict responsible gambling policies, offering features like GameSense, self-exclusion options, and deposit limits to help users gamble responsibly. But while PlayNow.com has strong security and social responsibility measures, it’s often criticized for its limited betting options, fewer promotional offers, and an overall less competitive user experience compared to the big international platforms.
Meanwhile, Bet365 and Betway are privately owned gambling giants with millions of users worldwide. Bet365, founded in 2000 in the UK, has over 63 million registered users and is licensed by the UK Gambling Commission and the Malta Gaming Authority. Betway, which started in 2006 in Malta, holds similar licenses but has put a stronger focus on sports sponsorships and esports betting, making it especially popular among younger users. Unlike PlayNow.com, these companies do not contribute any revenue to BC’s economy, since they operate outside the province’s jurisdiction. However, their biggest advantage is their superior betting experience—offering better odds, more sports betting markets, and a more user-friendly platform.
One of the biggest reasons people prefer Bet365 and Betway over PlayNow.com is their more advanced betting features. Bet365 offers live streaming, in-play betting, and an extensive selection of international sports leagues, making it a favorite for serious bettors. On the other hand, Betway specializes in esports betting, offering a more modern and tech-friendly betting experience. Both platforms also provide generous promotions, cashback offers, and enhanced odds, which PlayNow.com cannot compete with due to its strict regulatory limitations on promotions.
That said, Bet365 and Betway come with their own risks. Since they are not officially regulated by BC authorities, users don’t get the same consumer protections that PlayNow.com offers. These platforms don’t have the same legal obligations for responsible gambling, and if any disputes arise, users might not have the same financial protections they would with a government-backed site like PlayNow.com. Additionally, while these companies follow international regulations, their profits go to private shareholders rather than back into BC’s public services. So while Bet365 and Betway offer a much better gambling experience in terms of features, they don’t provide the same security or economic benefits as PlayNow.com.
