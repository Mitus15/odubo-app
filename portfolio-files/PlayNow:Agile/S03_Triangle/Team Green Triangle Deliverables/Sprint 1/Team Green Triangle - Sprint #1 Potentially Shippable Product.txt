 B. Competitive Analysis of BCLC’s PlayNow and Illegal Sportsbooks (Bet365, BetOnline)
British Columbia's (BC) internet sports betting market consists of regulated providers such as PlayNow, offered by the British Columbia Lottery Corporation (BCLC) and offshore websites such as Bet365 and BetOnline that provide services illegally to BC residents. 
Legal and Market Context
BCLC's PlayNow is BC's sole legal online sportsbook, generating CAD $341 million in net income (2023–2024) and funding public services like healthcare, education and community programs (BCLC Media Relations, 2024). Illegal offshore operators like Bet365 and BetOnline are dominating 51% of BC's online gambling market (Nightingale, 2025). These sites manage to control the international expertise through more favourable odds, live betting, and instant payouts to attract players. However, these sites expose players to risks like slowed payouts (Townsend & Mendick, 2017) and no legal recourse (Smyth, 2025) as they are operating without any legal framework.


Player Needs and Product Offerings
PlayNow focuses on responsible gambling tools such as mandated spending limits and self-exclusion to keep in alignment with BC's public health mandate (Government of British Columbia, 2015). However, its product offering lags behind its offshore competitors. For instance, PlayNow covers about 22 sports (PlayNow, n.d.) with limited live streaming of sports events, while Bet365 offers 40+ sports, along with massive live streaming of various sports (Bet365, n.d.). Similarly, BetOnline's cryptocurrency payouts complete withdrawals in under 24 hours (BetOnline, 2024), while PlayNow takes 3–5 business days via Interac (PlayNow, n.d.)
Odds of competitiveness also differ between the sites. PlayNow's provincial margins render the odds less competitive because, as a provincially regulated site, they have higher margins in their odds. This is partly due to the fact that they have to pay taxes and support community programs, and therefore the odds become less competitive than private sportsbooks' (Bryant, 2025). Bet365 is an international private sportsbook and has lower margins, which results in more competitive odds to obtain a broader base of users. For instance, Bet365 maintains a betting margin of approximately 5.68% on average, making it one of the more competitive in the market (Best Betting Sites in Canada, 2024).
Offshore operators also employ promotion aggressively; the 100% matching deposit bonus at Bet365 contrasts with the minimal promotions at PlayNow, a result of BC's strict advertising prohibitions. For instance, Bet365 offers Canadian new patrons a 100% matched bonus of as much as $200 for an initial deposit. That is, if a new patron deposits $200, they receive an additional $200 in bonus money, doubling what they have to wager (Smyth, 2025).
User Experience and Technology
PlayNow's mobile app, limits Android users and is criticized for its dated appearance and lack of iOS support (BC Sportsbook App, n.d.). Bet365, on the other hand, invests heavily in UX innovation in the form of live stats, bet builders, and personalized dashboards, and it has gained 80 million global users as a result (OddsShopper Sports Betting, 2025). BetOnline, though less polished, has functional cross-platform support that attracts tech-savvy bettors. PlayNow's lack of live streaming—a "must-have" feature for Bet365 patrons reduces its appeal for live games. YouGov research showed 71% of sports bettors aged 25-44 prefer having live streaming capability from their sportsbook apps (Mack, 2024). 
Strategic Recommendations for BCLC
BCLC must adopt international best practices while maintaining regulatory compliance to remain competitive. BCLC should partner with live-streaming platforms such as DAZN or TSN as this would increase user engagement and experience. Besides that, expanding different kinds of bets to include esports and proposition bets would allow PlayNow to engage with a wider audience. A loyalty program aligned with local teams could leverage regional pride and distinguish itself from offshore sites. BCLC is already partnered with the Vancouver Canucks, offering promotions like the "Canucks Fanuary Contest" with exclusive prizes like game tickets and autographed jerseys.  Expanding on such initiatives with a complete loyalty program would further enhance user engagement and loyalty (Canucks Fanuary Contest, n.d.). Finally, BCLC should also accelerate withdrawal speeds via Interac Instant, e-wallets, and cryptocurrency as this can address user complaints.  
C. Marketing Strategy Development for BCLC
The British Columbia Lottery Corporation (BCLC) plays a crucial role in the province’s gaming and lottery industry, offering everything from scratch tickets to online gaming through PlayNow.com. As the gambling landscape evolves with increasing digital competition and shifting consumer preferences, BCLC must refine its marketing strategy to remain relevant, attract new players, and promote responsible gaming.
This strategy outlines key approaches to enhance brand awareness, engage customers, and expand digital offerings while ensuring responsible gambling remains at the forefront of all initiatives.
Market Analysis
Industry Overview
The global gambling industry has experienced significant transformation, with online gaming and mobile gambling leading the charge. In Canada, the gaming market is tightly regulated, emphasizing consumer protection and responsible gambling (Canadian Gaming Association, 2023). The COVID-19 pandemic accelerated digital adoption, with more players opting for online gaming platforms.
Target Audience
BCLC’s marketing efforts must cater to a diverse audience, including:
	•	Casual Lottery Players: People who purchase tickets occasionally, primarily for major jackpot draws.
	•	Regular Gamers: Players engaged in both physical casino visits and online gaming.
	•	Younger Demographics (18-35): A tech-savvy audience drawn to mobile gaming, esports betting, and interactive gaming experiences.
	•	Responsible Gaming Advocates: Consumers looking for safe and regulated gambling options.
Competitor Analysis
BCLC competes with both government-regulated gaming organizations and private online gambling platforms. In BC, they only compete with the unregulated private online gambling platforms.
	•	Direct Competitors: Other provincial lottery corporations like Ontario Lottery and Gaming (OLG) and Alberta Gaming, Liquor & Cannabis (AGLC).
	•	Indirect Competitors: International and offshore gambling websites, such as Bet365 and DraftKings, which provide unregulated betting options.
BCLC’s competitive advantage lies in its trustworthiness, contributions to the province, and strict adherence to responsible gaming standards (BCLC, 2024).
 
Marketing Objectives
To stay ahead in the evolving gaming industry, BCLC’s marketing strategy can focus on:
	•	Boosting Brand Awareness: Strengthening its presence across digital and traditional platforms.
	•	Increasing Player Engagement: Encouraging participation through interactive promotions and personalized offers.
	•	Expanding Digital Reach: Enhancing PlayNow.com and mobile gaming options.
	•	Promoting Responsible Gaming: Positioning BCLC as a leader in ethical gambling.
	•	Driving Revenue Growth: Ensuring profitability while adhering to regulatory requirements.
Marketing Strategies
Digital Marketing & Online Expansion
With online gaming on the rise, BCLC must strengthen its digital presence through:
	•	SEO-Optimized Content: Creating engaging blogs and game guides to boost organic traffic.
	•	Social Media & Influencer Marketing: Leveraging platforms like Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube for interactive campaigns.
	•	Mobile App Enhancements: Improving the PlayNow app with seamless navigation, exclusive mobile promotions, and personalized notifications.
Personalized Customer Engagement
BCLC can enhance player experiences with:
	•	Loyalty Programs: Offering incentives such as free plays, discounts, and VIP perks for repeat customers.
	•	Data-Driven Email & SMS Marketing: Sending customized promotions based on user preferences and behaviors.
	•	AI Chatbots & Customer Support: Providing real-time assistance and responsible gambling resources.
Traditional Advertising & Brand Positioning
	•	TV & Radio Ads: Running engaging commercials showcasing jackpot winners and community contributions.
	•	Billboard & Transit Ads: Promoting major lottery draws in high-traffic areas.
	•	Sponsorships & Events: Partnering with local sports teams and festivals to increase brand visibility.
  
Responsible Gaming Initiatives
BCLC’s reputation relies on prioritizing responsible gambling through:
	•	Public Awareness Campaigns: Educating players on safe gambling habits via online and offline channels.
	•	Self-Exclusion Programs: Strengthening tools that help players manage their gambling behavior.
	•	Collaborations with Mental Health Organizations: Partnering with experts to offer resources and support for problem gambling (Responsible Gambling Council, 2023).
Expansion into New Markets
	•	New Game Development: Introducing interactive lottery games and esports betting options to attract younger audiences.
	•	Retail & Cross-Promotions: Enhancing partnerships with retailers to increase in-store lottery ticket sales.
	•	Strategic Collaborations: Working with other regulated gaming platforms for exclusive promotions and joint incentives.
Performance Measurement & KPIs
To track success, BCLC should monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), such as:
	•	Website Traffic & Engagement: Measuring visits, session duration, and conversion rates on PlayNow.com.
	•	Lottery & Casino Sales Growth: Evaluating year-over-year revenue increases.
	•	Social Media Metrics: Analyzing follower growth, engagement rates, and campaign effectiveness.
	•	Customer Retention & Loyalty: Tracking repeat participation in promotions and loyalty programs.
	•	Responsible Gaming Impact: Assessing the effectiveness of awareness campaigns and support initiatives.
BCLC has a unique opportunity to strengthen its market position by embracing digital innovation, personalizing customer interactions, and championing responsible gaming. By executing this strategy, BCLC can attract new players, retain existing customers, and continue generating revenue that benefits communities across British Columbia.
